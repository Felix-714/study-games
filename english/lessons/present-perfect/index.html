🌱 園丁的完成式溫室

Present Perfect 小教室 · 先種好規則，再收成答案

1. 核心種植公式

Subject + has / have + past participle
  • He / She / It / 單數 用 has
  • I / You / We / They / 複數 用 have

例：The gardener has planted the flowers.

2. 點解要用完成式？

用嚟講一件過去發生，但同而家有關嘅事情。

  • The storm has broken the window.
  • Chelsea has found the key.
  • We have finished the cipher.

3. 常見不規則種子

原形Past participle
hithit, 不是 hitted
writewritten, 不是 writeed
breakbroken, 不是 breaked
findfound
seeseen
leaveleft

4. 園丁的檢查方法

  1. 先圈主語，決定 has / have
  2. 再搵動詞原形
  3. 轉做past participle
  4. 最後讀一次，確認唔係普通過去式

錯：The storm has hitted the garden.
啱：The storm has hit the garden.

5. 溫室小測

The gardener ______ the broken flower pot. (find)
